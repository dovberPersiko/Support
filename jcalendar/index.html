JCalendar

A Hebrew calendar for iPhone: dates, holidays, the weekly parsha, Daf Yomi, and daily zmanim (candle lighting, sunset, nightfall and more) calculated for your place. The app is named לחיות עם הזמן on the App Store.

Features

  • Month view with Hebrew and civil dates, holidays, fasts and Rosh Chodesh.
  • Weekly parsha and Daf Yomi.
  • Zmanim for your current location or any place you choose.
  • Hebrew and English interface.

Support

Questions, a wrong date or time, or a bug? Write to dober.p@gmail.com. Please include the app version (Settings → About), your iOS version, and the date and place involved.

Frequently asked

Why does the app ask for my location?
To calculate sunset, candle lighting and other zmanim for where you are. You can decline and pick a city by hand instead. See the privacy policy.
How are candle lighting and nightfall calculated?
Candle lighting is 18 minutes before sunset (40 minutes in the Jerusalem area). Nightfall and havdalah are 20 minutes after sunset. Other times use the defaults of the open source zmanim library.
Which open source code does the app use?
See Open source & LGPL, which also explains how to get the source code and replace the LGPL library.
