Privacy policy

JCalendar has no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking. The developer does not run a server that receives your data.

What the app uses

Location

If you allow it, the app reads your approximate or precise location while you use it, only to calculate zmanim and candle lighting for where you are. The coordinates are used on your device. To show the name of the place, the coordinates are sent to Apple's system mapping service, which is covered by Apple's privacy policy. If you decline, you can choose a city by hand.

Place search

When you search for a place, the text you type is sent to Apple's system mapping service (MapKit) to find matches. The developer does not receive it.

Data stored on your device

Your chosen place and your settings are saved on your device only. Deleting the app removes them.

What the app does not do

  • It does not collect your name, email, contacts, photos or identifiers.
  • It does not track you across apps or websites, and it contains no advertising or analytics code.
  • It does not sell or share data, because it does not collect any.

Children

The app is not directed at children and collects no personal data from anyone.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here with a new date.
